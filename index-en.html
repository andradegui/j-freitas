About us

The J.Freitas Importation and Exportation is a Brazilian trading company specialized in the export of automotive parts for replacement, operating exclusively in the passenger car and truck segments.

Founded in 2011, the company was established with the purpose of serving distributors located mainly in Germany. Over the years, it has expanded its operations and now supplies various countries in Europe, South America, the United States and Africa.

With over 40 years of experience accumulated in the automotive industry, our founders have transformed knowledge and dedication into lasting commercial relationships.

We offer access to the best Brazilian suppliers, combining excellent quality, competitive pricing, and logistical agility, to meet the most demanding needs of the international market.

Operation and Market

We operate in the B2B (Business to Business) model, purchasing parts on demand from Brazilian suppliers and exporting them to international distributors.

Our profile includes parts for vehicles Volkswagen (Fusca and Kombi), GM, Ford and trucks, covering body components, engine, sheet metal and mechanical parts.

With a focus on flexibility and customization, we offer solutions that meet the preferences and requirements of each costumer, from neutral packaging to customized logistics processes.
